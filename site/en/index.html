The exam does not ask for the definition.
It asks for the right decision under constraints.

This platform is preparation material for SDAIA’s Expert AI Engineer badge exam, built on the seven published exam domains and their weights. It consists of 12 units: Unit 01 gives you the solving method and the calculations that serve every question, Units 02–11 cover the seven domains, and Unit 12 brings together the case studies and the final review. Each unit ends with an applied scenario that imitates the exam questions and with review questions with explained answers. It is preparation material drawn from candidates’ reports and published SDAIA releases, and it is not an official curriculum issued by SDAIA.

From reading the scenario to choosing the cheapest sufficient solution.

The platform is designed for candidates for SDAIA’s Expert AI Engineer badge: engineers who work, or are preparing to work, on building, deploying and operating AI systems, and who need to make technical decisions that weigh cost, latency, accuracy and compliance together. The material assumes basic working knowledge of machine learning and software, and focuses on choosing between reasonable solutions more than on definitions.

1

Understand and decide with numbers

  • Extract the hard and soft requirements from the scenario text before reading the options.
  • Calculate latency, capacity, replica count and the expected cost of a threshold.
  • Choose the metric that fits the cost of error, not overall accuracy.
2

Build and operate

  • Design RAG that stays fresh, and serving interfaces that are resilient and secure.
  • Choose the right deployment, monitoring and retraining strategy.
  • Scale a service on GPUs inside the Kingdom.
3

Govern and comply

  • Name precisely the SDAIA principle or PDPL obligation that was violated.
  • Spot the waste in a bill and propose the cheapest sufficient alternative.
  • Reach an integrated decision in case studies drawn from the Saudi workplace.

Don’t just read. Solve the scenario.

If you are a beginner, start with the primer, Unit 00. In every section, read “In Plain Terms” and the definitions first, then the detail. Work through the units in order, then cover the decision in the applied scenario and rebuild it from the requirements. After that, open the exam-style questions, and do not move on until you know why each wrong option fails.
